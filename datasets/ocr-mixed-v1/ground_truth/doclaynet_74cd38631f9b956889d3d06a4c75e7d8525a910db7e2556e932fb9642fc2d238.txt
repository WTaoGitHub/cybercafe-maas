6.3.7
FlashSystem 900 and client VMware ESX hosts
The FlashSystem 900 can be attached to VMware ESX client hosts by using the FC method.
Arbitrated loop topology is required when you attach the IBM FlashSystem 900 directly to the 
client VMware ESX hosts.
Note:
 The FlashSystem 900 16 Gbps FC attachment does not support arbitrated loop 
topology. The IBM FlashSystem 900 must be connected to a SAN switch when 16 Gbps 
FC is used if the host operating system does not support point-to-point FC direct 
connections. If arbitrated loop is required by the client host, connect at 8 Gbps FC to the 
IBM FlashSystem 900.
