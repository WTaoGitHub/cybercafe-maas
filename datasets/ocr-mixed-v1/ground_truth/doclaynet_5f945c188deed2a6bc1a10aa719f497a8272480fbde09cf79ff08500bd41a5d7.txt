Brazilian Tax Issue
From 2002 to 2007, the Company’s Brazilian subsidiary imported goods through the State of Espirito Santo
in Brazil and subsequently transferred them to its facility in the State of Rio de Janeiro. During that period, the
Company’s Brazilian subsidiary paid taxes to the State of Espirito Santo on its imports. Upon the final sale of
these goods, the Company’s Brazilian subsidiary collected taxes from customers and remitted them to the State
of Rio de Janeiro net of the taxes paid on importation of those goods to the State of Espirito Santo in accordance
with the Company’s understanding of Brazilian tax laws.
